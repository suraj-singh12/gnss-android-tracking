Cross-platform, offline-first operational location tracking

      

        An offline-first GNSS tracking system in which Android devices collect
        and retain location observations, while a local Command Centre receives
        positions, displays movement and historical tracks, manages recording
        and handles emergency SOS alerts over a local network.
