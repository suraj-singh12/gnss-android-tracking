Phone alert

Alert expiry: 5–3600 seconds. Normal output is attempted once; its notification remains available until expiry/Stop. Urgent output stops at expiry, duration, local Stop or confirmed cancellation. Sound/vibration depend on Android settings; output attempted does not mean heard. Phone alerts are separate from SOS.

GNSS Tracker & GNSS Command Centre
